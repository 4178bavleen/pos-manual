1. Introduction

1.1 What is Bhojan Bandhu POS?

Bhojan Bandhu POS is a cloud-based point-of-sale and restaurant management system for restaurants, cafés, QSRs, bakeries and cloud kitchens that operate as single outlets or as multi-branch franchises.

It replaces the paper KOT, the billing register, the stock diary and the spreadsheet. Staff take orders on screen, the kitchen sees them instantly on a display, receipts and KOTs print on thermal printers, stock is deducted automatically, and the owner sees live sales — from one system.

The software runs entirely in a web browser. There is no installation required for the main application; only the optional local print helper (see Section 19) is installed on the billing computer.

1.2 Who uses it?

User Typical day
Platform administrator (Super Admin) Manages franchises, plans, integrations, platform settings
Franchise owner Watches all branches, menus, offers, subscriptions, reports
Branch manager Runs the outlet: orders, tables, staff, inventory, reports
Cashier Bills customers, takes payments, handles refunds, manages the cash drawer
Kitchen staff Works the kitchen display: accepts, cooks, and marks orders ready
Inventory manager Tracks stock, vendors, purchase orders, deliveries
Accountant Revenue, expenses, GST, refunds, settlements, P&L

1.3 What problems does it solve?

  • Faster billing — take and settle an order in seconds from one screen, with keyboard shortcuts.
  • No lost tickets — orders flow from counter to kitchen screen in real time; KOTs print automatically.
  • Controlled stock — ingredients are deducted from inventory when an order enters preparation; low stock raises alerts.
  • Multi-branch visibility — owners compare branches; managers see only their own outlet.
  • Correct taxation — GST is calculated server-side from configured tax rules; inclusive/exclusive handling; GST summaries.
  • Channel unification — Swiggy, Zomato, website, WhatsApp and QR orders arrive on one board.
  • Works during internet outages — the POS keeps billing offline and reconciles later.
  • Accountability — every create/change/delete is written to an audit log; deleted records go to a Recycle Bin before permanent removal.

1.4 Key capabilities

  • Full billing terminal (POS) with dine-in, takeaway and delivery-platform order types
  • Kitchen Display System (KDS) with real-time tickets and recipe-based stock deduction
  • Menu, combos, offers/coupons, taxes
  • Table management, reservations, QR contactless ordering, public order tracking
  • Inventory, vendors, purchase orders (with Input GST/ITC tracking), branch transfers
  • Cash drawer sessions, expenses, refunds/voids, GST compliance dashboard
  • Staff: employees, shifts, attendance, timesheet reports
  • Reports & analytics, branch and franchise comparison, CSV export
  • Online channel integration (Swiggy, Zomato, Website, WhatsApp), settlements and monitoring
  • Users, roles and fine-grained permissions; audit logs; recycle bin; encrypted backups
  • Thermal printing (80 mm receipts and KOTs), branding, receipt customization
  • Offline billing with sync/reconciliation; real-time notifications

Bhojan Bandhu POS is browser-based. Requirements:

Accounts and access

  • A user account must be created for you by your administrator (Section 18.1).
  • No additional third-party accounts are required for core billing.
  • Optional integrations may require: Razorpay/Stripe (payments), SMTP mail server (emails), Meta WhatsApp Business account (WhatsApp messages), Swiggy/Zomato API credentials (delivery channels).

ℹ️ Note: Always keep at least one billing terminal able to reach the server. Offline orders are stored locally on that terminal and must be reconciled afterwards (Section 18.13).

Step 1: Open Chrome or Edge on your computer.

Step 2: In the address bar, type the web address given to you by your administrator (for example your restaurant’s POS URL) and press Enter.

Step 3: The login screen appears.

Wrong password

  • After 5 failed attempts within 15 minutes, the account (and the network address) is temporarily blocked and you will see an error telling you to retry after some time. Wait for the cool-down window and try again, or ask your administrator to reset your password (Section 18.1).

Account states

Account deactivated (INACTIVE) or deleted Login refused with an access message
Franchise suspended Only the platform Super Admin can log in
Franchise inactive The owner sees the system read-only; branch staff cannot log in

3.3 Remember me and sessions

  • The Remember me checkbox stores your session on that device.
  • Sessions expire after 1 day. When it expires you are returned to the login screen — this is normal; simply log in again.
  • Logging out from another device or by an administrator ends your session too.

3.4 Forgot password / password recovery

Supported recovery path (recommended):

Step 1: Contact your franchise administrator (branch manager or franchise owner).

Step 2: They open Users, find your account, click Reset Password and give you the new password (Section 18.1).

Step 3: Log in with the new password and keep it secret.

On the login screen, clicking Forgot password? shows the message: “Please contact your franchise administrator to reset your credentials.”

ℹ️ Note: Pages /forgot-password and /reset-password also exist and can be opened by typing the address directly after /, but they are not linked from the login screen and may not be enabled for your deployment. The administrator reset above is the reliable path.

3.5 Logging out

Step 1: Click your name/avatar area at the bottom of the sidebar.

Step 2: Click Logout.

Step 3: You are returned to the login screen. Local cart and session data on that browser are cleared.

🔐 Security: Log out at the end of every shift, especially on shared counter computers. Alternatively, use the app lock (below) which keeps you logged in but locks the screen.

3.6 App lock (lock your screen instantly)

Lock the application without logging out — useful when you step away from the counter.

Step 1: Press Ctrl + L (Windows/Linux) or ⌘ + L (Mac), or click the lock icon in the top header.

Step 2: The screen is covered by the lock overlay.

Step 3: To continue, type your account password and confirm. After several wrong attempts the unlock is temporarily blocked.

ℹ️ Note: The lock state survives a page refresh. Public pages (login, order tracking) stay reachable while locked. Administrators can also assign a 4-digit app PIN to a user from the Users page (Set PIN).

3.7 Self-registration and onboarding (new franchises)

New franchise owners can sign up themselves:

Step 1: Open the /register page (registration is not linked from the login screen; your onboarding contact will give you the link).

Step 2: Enter your email and password (or continue with Google), then confirm.

Step 3: Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your email. You have 10 minutes; you may enter it up to 5 times before it expires.

Step 4: Choose a subscription plan and pay the order (Razorpay/Stripe).

Step 5: After payment is verified, your franchise, first branch and administrator account are created and you can log in.

📞 Questions during onboarding: WhatsApp +91 8699524005 (also shown on the login screen).

3.8 First-time setup checklist

For a new installation, the administrator should complete these in order:

  1. Log in as the administrator account.
  2. Confirm franchise/branch details: name, address, phone, GST number (Settings → Franchise & Branch; Branches page).
  3. Set receipt layout: logo, header, footer, FSSAI, paper size (Receipt Settings).
  4. Configure tax rules and turn GST on for the branch (Tax & Compliance).
  5. Create categories and menu items with prices (Menu).
  6. Set units and inventory items with reorder/critical levels (Inventory).
  7. Add vendors (Vendors) and create the first purchase order if needed.
  8. Add tables (Table Management).
  9. Create users and assign roles (Users) — one per person, never shared.
  10. Review User Roles permission switches if your operations differ from the defaults.
  11. Configure payment methods shown on the billing terminal (Settings → Payment Methods).
  12. Install the Churi Print Service on billing computers and test-print (Section 19).
  13. Open the cash drawer with an opening balance to start trading (Cash Drawer).
  14. Run one test order end to end: bill → KOT → kitchen → payment → receipt.
  15. Take a backup snapshot (Backup & Recovery) once setup is complete.

Step 4: Click Save to apply the changed permissions.

7.7 Taxes on the bill

  • Tax is calculated by the server from the tax rules configured under Tax & Compliance, never typed by the cashier.
  • Rules are resolved branch-first, then franchise-level. A branch-specific rule with the same name overrides the franchise rule.
  • Rule fields: tax type (GST/VAT/Service/Custom), rate, apply-on (all items / category / item), inclusive or exclusive of price, active flag.
  • If a branch has GST disabled, no GST component is applied to its orders.
  • The bill stores a tax breakdown (per component/rate) which prints on the receipt (CGST/SGST split).

Default on a fresh installation: one tenant-level rule — GST 5% on all items, exclusive of price.

ℹ️ Note: Cashiers can view tax (permission tax.view) but only administrators can change tax rules (tax.manage).

7.14 Offline billing

If the internet drops:

  • The terminal keeps working — menu and cached data come from local storage.
  • New orders are queued on that device with a unique offline ID.
  • When connectivity returns, queued orders sync automatically; duplicates are impossible because each offline order has a unique ID (a re-send returns the existing order instead of creating a second one).
  • Track the queue at PLATFORM/OFFLINE → Offline Orders (Offline Reconciliation, Section 18.13).

⚠️ Warning: Do not clear browser data/IndexedDB while offline orders are pending — you would lose the queue.

19.1 How printing works

Printing uses a three-tier fallback — the first available tier wins:

1 Churi Print Service (local helper app) Service installed & running on the billing PC Silent print — no browser dialog
2 QZ Tray QZ Tray + Java installed; printer name saved in the app settings (churi_printer_name) Silent print

20. Offline Operations

The system is designed to keep billing during internet outages.

During an outage:

  • The POS continues to work from cached menu/inventory data.
  • Orders are stored locally on that terminal with a unique offline ID.
  • Held bills remain local (Section 7.10).

When connectivity returns:

  • Queued orders sync automatically (Offline Sessions → Sync).
  • The backend re-agrees the outage window (about a 60-minute grace) and deduplicates by offline ID — a repeated submission returns the original order instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Terminals send periodic heartbeats so you can see which are online.

Monitoring: Offline Orders page shows the sync queue, server sessions (duration, order count, gross total) and session details.

Branch configuration: each branch has offline orders enabled and an offline threshold hours setting; offline mode reads branch configuration from the public branch-config endpoint.

⚠️ Warning: Do not clear browser storage while offline orders are pending. Reconcile the queue the same day so revenue and stock stay accurate.

22. Error Reference

Invalid credentials / login refused

Meaning: Wrong email/username or password; or the account is inactive/deleted; or the franchise is suspended. Solution: Check the identifier (username or email works). Wait if you recently failed 5 times (15-minute cool-down). Ask your administrator to Reset Password. If it continues: provide your username, approximate time and the exact message to support.

429 Too Many Requests (retry after X seconds)

Meaning: Login rate limit — 5 failed attempts per account or 100 per network address within 15 minutes. Solution: Wait for the shown retry time; use the correct password; avoid repeated attempts from shared computers.

Access Denied / Forbidden on a page

Meaning: Your role lacks the permission for that path, or you are acting on another branch’s data. Solution: Return to the dashboard and use the sidebar items shown for your role. If you need the page, ask an administrator to enable the permission in User Roles.

Franchise Inactive (Read-Only Mode) banner / BILLING SUSPENDED / OPERATIONS SUSPENDED

Meaning: The franchise is inactive (subscription/licence issue). Viewing is allowed, changes are blocked. Solution: Franchise owner should open Subscriptions and renew/reactivate. Branch staff should contact the owner.

Session expired

Meaning: The login token expired (sessions last 1 day) or you logged out elsewhere. Solution: Log in again. Check the last order in Orders before re-billing.

Coupon/offer not applied

Meaning: Code is inactive, expired, usage limit reached, below the minimum order amount, not valid for this branch, or the percentage is capped by max discount. Solution: Verify the offer in Offers & Discounts; bill without the coupon if invalid.

Order total cannot be less than amount already paid

Meaning: You tried to reduce an order below its captured payment. Solution: Keep the payment as credit, or cancel the order and issue a refund (Sections 8.4, 15.3).

Refund rejected: Self-approval not allowed

Meaning: The person who created a refund cannot approve it. Solution: Another authorised user (manager/accountant) approves it.

Payment gateway not configured

Meaning: No active Razorpay/Stripe configuration (needed for subscription payments and guest online payments). Solution: Super Admin configures the payment integration (Section 18.11) and tests it.

Stock deduction did not happen

Meaning: The item has no recipe configured, or stock was already deducted (each order deducts only once), or stock is at zero. Solution: Add recipe ingredients to the menu item; check the item’s History for existing movements.

Duplicate/missing offline orders

Meaning: Orders taken during an outage are still in the local queue, or a sync is in progress. Solution: Open Offline Orders and trigger sync; each offline order’s unique ID guarantees it is created exactly once.

404 — Page Not Found

Meaning: The address is wrong, or you followed a link to a page that does not exist in this build (see Section 32). Solution: Use the sidebar navigation instead of editing URLs.

Print dialog appears / nothing prints

See Section 19.6.

INTEGRATION ORDER FAILED notification

Meaning: An incoming channel order could not be processed (credentials, mapping or provider outage). Solution: Check Online Channels → Monitor/Logs, fix mapping/credentials, then re-enter the order manually from Online Orders → New Online Order so the customer is served.

24. Security Best Practices

🔐 Passwords - Use a long, unique password per person. Never share it. - Default/demo passwords shipped with seed data must be changed on first login — treat them as public. - Never write passwords on the counter, on receipts, or in group chats.

🔐 Accounts are personal - Never share one login between cashiers — refunds, voids and audit entries must be attributable. - Deactivate a user immediately when they leave (Users → status = INACTIVE).

🔐 Sessions - Lock the app (Ctrl/⌘ + L) when you step away; log out fully at shift end. - Avoid Remember me on public/shared computers.

🔐 Permissions - Grant the least permission that lets someone do their job (edit switches in User Roles). - Do not hand Super Admin credentials to operators.

🔐 Sensitive data - API keys, SMTP passwords, WhatsApp tokens and gateway secrets live encrypted in the system — never copy them into tickets, emails or screenshots. - Customer phone numbers are personal data — use exports and bulk messaging responsibly.

🔐 Payments - Never ask a customer to share card details/OTP. - Reconcile the cash drawer daily; investigate variances promptly.

🔐 Admin operations - Take a snapshot before restores, bulk imports, tax changes or menu bulk uploads. - Review Audit Logs regularly for unexpected changes.

🔐 Suspicious activity - Unexpected login prompts, strange orders, or unknown users in Audit Logs → change passwords, disable the account, and contact support (Section 28).

26. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? Ask your administrator — Users → Reset Password (Section 18.1). The login screen’s Forgot password? link explains this.

How does a manager create a new user? Users → Add User → fill name, username, email, password, role, branch → Save (Section 18.1).

Why can’t I see a feature? Your role does not have that permission. Menus are hidden automatically. An administrator can enable it in User Roles.

How do I export data? Use Data Export (choose type + date range) or the export buttons on Reports/Attendance/Audit pages.

What happens if my internet fails? Billing continues; orders queue locally on that terminal and sync automatically when back online. Reconcile at Offline Orders.

How do I cancel an operation/order? Orders → open the order → Cancel with a reason. If it was paid, a refund request is created automatically. To discard an unsent cart, use Void Cart with a reason.

How do I start/end a shift with cash? Open the drawer with an opening balance (Cash Drawer or POS Open Drawer); close it with the counted amount to see the variance.

How do I print a duplicate receipt? Open the order and use its receipt/view action (pos.access permission), then print.

How do I add a discount? Type a coupon code (Offers) at billing, or use the discount field. The server validates it.

How do I seat a reservation? Reservations → find the booking → Seat Guest.

How do I move an order to another table? Table Management → Move KOT / Transfer Table.

How do I contact support? WhatsApp/phone +91 8699524005 (linked from the login screen, POS and Help Center), or the Help Center ticket form (Section 28).

Can I use the POS on a tablet/phone? The layout adapts to smaller screens, but a desktop/laptop is recommended for busy counters.

Where are held bills stored? Locally in that browser/device only (Section 7.10).

How do I know who changed a price? Check Audit Logs (search by module/date).

How do I stop an inactive franchise from billing? It is automatic: inactive franchises show BILLING SUSPENDED; suspend/reactivate from the Franchise page (Super Admin).

32. Known Limitations

Documented honestly so users are not surprised (as verified in the current build):

  1. Forgot-password self-service is not wired to the login screen — the supported path is an administrator reset. The /forgot-password and /reset-password pages exist but are reachable only by direct URL.
  2. Registration page (/register) is not linked from the login screen; onboarding links are supplied by the vendor.
  3. Held bills are device-local — they do not travel between terminals.
  4. Some dashboard footer links (e.g. a POS history link, a GST shortcut) point to addresses that are not registered pages and will show Page Not Found — navigate from the sidebar instead.
  5. /stock-adjustment, /delivery and /ad-builder have no sidebar entries (use direct URLs or the in-page actions); the /delivery address currently shows the Data Export screen.
  6. SMS is not supported — notifications are in-app, email and WhatsApp.
  7. The Ad Creative Builder and AI Expansion outputs depend on configured data sources and are advisory.
  8. Wildcards such as [Support Email] and [Support Business Hours] must be completed by your organisation before distribution.
